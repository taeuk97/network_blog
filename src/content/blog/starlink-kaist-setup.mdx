On **20 Nov 2026** we bought a Starlink Standard Kit and installed it on a rooftop railing at KAIST.

<Figure src="/images/blog/starlink-kaist-setup/starlink-terminal.jpg" alt="Starlink Standard Kit dish mounted on a rooftop railing at KAIST in light snow" caption="The Starlink terminal on the KAIST rooftop." width={520} />

Behind the terminal, a Mac mini runs a RIPE Atlas software probe — [probe #1019197](https://atlas.ripe.net/probes/1019197/), "Starlink KAIST ACAI" — which came online on 22 Nov. With it, we've started ping and traceroute measurements over Starlink.

Results will come in a later post once enough data has been collected.
